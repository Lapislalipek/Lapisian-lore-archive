Here is covered everything on how Lapisians treat eachother. What they do for fun, what they do by themselves, how they react to surroundings and more.

Behavior

Lapisians and other beings

Lapisians are mainly calm and wouldn't harm anyone without a proper reason to do so. Although thats true, they do not seem to like any interactions with any human like creatures whatsoever not having any trust towards them. Gaining trust is a really tedious and hard job especially if you arent really trustfull. Gainging trust requires you to act super friendly and not raise any reasons for them to be suspicious or feel uneasy around you. This could be hard but real honour is getting their full trust. What that means is that they trust you fully with all their hearts and will basically do anything for you, won't hide anything and will let you interact with younger lapisians. Gaining this type of trust is almost impossible due to how they react to strangers but the "easiest way" to do so is sacrificing something really important for them or save their live in a very stressful situations. This might give a chance to get their trust.

Loyalty and affection

Lapisians are very loyal to eachother, and if they do feel super strong affection towards another one, they may become lovers. When this happens, a bond forms between them - similarily how this happens between healers and guardians. They both go through a natural process of bonding which (just as in healers case) tunes their heads to recognize the heartbeat of their lover. It can only happen with help of the affection - it lets Lapisians preform that process (this is the only way).

Bonding perks

This "perk" I'd say, is used to care for eachother in a much more effective way. Both lovers know when their pair is feeling down or is in some sort of discomfort by simply just feeling that something isn't right (again, just like healers do). As I mentioned, loyalty is something they do have towards eachother and they do not break up. Well.. at least they should not. This can be more annoying and dangerous at the same time: First of all - stress and sadness, second of all - bonding is still there. This process is not going to just randomly disappear, it stays as long as they live - of course they can re-bond with someone else but it will take much much longer than the first time and may not be so effective. This weakens the bond, making the relationship more troubling. The bonding process that happens for healers is likely one way healer->guardian not the opposite way. They are bonded together (healers) but guardians arent bonded to healers

Special occasions treatment

There's no real official announcement Lapisians got bonded, its a very personal thing and even asking in a matter different than health related topics when lapisians bonded is considered rude and invasive to their personal space. Capsule giving birth are very important, they do welcome every new society member very warmly since life is a very important gift they cherish. Its not something big and loud, they preferably like peace, making sure the young Lapisian has a smooth start into his precious life.

Gem Guardians

Bittersweet reality of Guardians

Guardians are the only hope of other Lapisians, they truly rely on him. That is why they are ready do absolutely anything they can do to aid him in even the smallest problems of this stressful life. Guardians are loved and respected to their fullest by everyone. Since they have very strong minds they can really endure a TON of pressure (like VERY VERY huge amounts). This can crack a normal Lapisian with ease, they stay strong through all of that. Relationships are truly a wonderful thing around to have, a guardian can and should find himself a partner. Though I said, there isn't too much time for actual feelings due to the job they need to do. That partner should be responsible to get the Guardian to feel more loved, that's what she is going to do (guardians are mostly males, but females may happen occasionally, RARE though they are mostly healers). The moments they have together are priceless and they use them to their full potential - this is the only moment a guardian can relieve himself emotionally if he needs to. That way he can be more productive and mentally stronger. And if even a crack happens, this poor soul will be calmed down ASAP. Bond between Healers and Guardians is so strong (bonding process, can tak about it more if you wish to) that healers will sense this immidately - they drop everything they do and rush to help. It can take a long time to calm him down but when he cries, everything can go down so this is more than urgent this is NECESSARY for their lives to continue on.

Guardian relationships

Guardian-Lapisian relationships may occur, but Lapisians shouldnt interfere with his feelings. If he decides to actually find a loving partner, he should do it himself. The chosen one is likely to have more safety guaranteed but less time to express her feelings towards him. Although when it occurs it is much stronger that other relationships. This can act as psychological support and often acts like one. Unstable guardians are either badly damaged physically or mentally by some occurring problems. That's rare but happens sadly. . .

Guardians approach to praise and constant help

Guardian most of the times won't ask anybody for praising or anything apart from basic needs he requires. Lapisians however being very generous (as said before) will do anything they can in their power to make the guardian happy, when in reality all he desires is really just seeing them happy and smile. "Your smile is the most precious gift you can give him, show him his work has impact on your everyday life" Guardians belive this is this is all they need from others (bonded partners excluded), humbleness is their proud personality perk they won't hide. Although if they do get praised in special ways, Guardians treat these very seriously and they find them extremely precious. They value all support they get from everyone, they see them trying and he won't stop them unless they risk themselves getting hurt.

Mental imbalances

Gem guardians will never ever show themselves crying. If the situation is so bad they do, they isolate themselves. Healers are spiritually bond to them and they will immidiately understand something is wrong. First instinct is to rush for help and that's what they are programmed to do. Drops everything and rushes back. Guardians don't want the panic to spread to others, because if he cries that means that the problem is so huge even they cannot help with the stress. Not having anyone above you is a both sided sword - The higher you climb the less things you can grab onto Guardians are on the top, which means the only support is from the bottom, therefore there is no one to just hold everything together when they have fallen. The constant pressure that they are responsible for the rest of the hideouts safety plus the fact that they cannot rely on anyone else than themselves in support is genuienly the biggest psychological challenge they can face. That's why in all that chaos they are the ones to take lead even tho Lapisians don't have set leaders. Gem guardians are the type not to say "Come with me", they are going to say "Don't worry, you are safe". Loved by all, supported by everyone, known everywhere, on the top of the rank, quietly lonely, always suffer, always laugh, always life.

The great loss..

When a guardian dies... Healers are going to completely freak out, they are in a critical state: They become disoriented, hopeless, terrified, sad, EVERYTHING that can possibly affect them negatively may happen, their bond with him will send thousands of shots per second, it will genuienly bombard their heads with very uncomfortable feelings. It will not be a suprise if they aggresively burst into tears one by one, they are going to sacrifice every last bit of hope, energy just to try and save him, bring him back to life. There is no more calming down, there is no more stability, just one goal, which they cannot achieve, because it's already lost... In aftermath the hideout is generally speaking in a very terrific state, they will not panic, not everyone , they know what happened, they know the consequences, but if they all panic it's over, they desperately will look for another guardian everywhere, they will do everything to find someone that can protect them (and capsules of course), they may even go for a lookout of another hideout in hope of another guardian being there, they NEED him to survive, so this is crucial, they will not leave everything behind - they can move, but capsules can't. Leaving them back there is a death sentence for a whole generations and will further decrease the population in the future, they CANNOT afford another huge loss... If they manage, the hideout is safe, they will remember the fallen hero, forever If they don't, may fate itself help them . . .

Feelings from the other side

For starters this is a huge tragedy, once a guardian died everything falls apart as I said... the lover will suddenly feel something is heavily wrong, won't know exactly what (similar to how they feel when guardian is scared) until about a minute later. Then an uncomfortable feeling turns into an unbearable pain. They fall on the ground as their whole chest hurts uncontrollably, they curl in tears as they cannot relieve it. It hurts, it hurts so bad they can't breathe. Then suddenly a huge relief comes as pain goes away, and with it...

Silence...

No heartbeat, no feeling of partners presence... nothing. Complete void. The partner will likely shake, tears literally flooding everything, desperate attempt, regain the feeling of safety. Whole energy will perish with the moment as the lover tries pointlessly pushing everything in their power to save him. But all for nothing...

Other

What not to say

When engaging in conversations, all subjects are welcome, personal needs, problems, all welcome and even necessary. Lapisians (especially ones closer to eachother) need to know these things, since they're not bonded with them, verbal and visual suggestions something is off is what they need. When it comes to things no Lapisian is allowed to ask couples is their bonding times. Question type "when did you last bond?" is considered rude and sort of break into their private space. This rule doesn't apply to conversations where this does make a key difference for a life matter, then all rules are overriden like these. Mainly these conversations will take place between healers.

What happens when a Lapisian kills one another

Version 1:

Lapisian getting hurt accidentaly

In this case, most likely the Lapisian responsible for that will help and react quickly avoiding anything in particular that could go wrong with the Lapisian further.

Version 2:

Lapisian getting hurt on purpose

In this case things get pretty different, because the victim is treated first (always if possible) then the rest, therefore if such situation happens one Lapisian will be responsible for the safety of the victim and another one will take action towards the attacker. In that case they will face consequences of these actions. They will either get disposed from the hideout or they may go under procedures that will check their sanity levels and persumably reroll the energy to simply revive the normal behavior.

Version 3:

Lapisian kills accidentaly

In that case, things are pretty straightforward. Whoever did this reacts immidiately and if it is possible, tries everything to save him. Tho he doesn't manage, the Lapisian dies and now the not fun part begins. First of all they will get escorted to safety if needed (dead one) and the one that killed is still responsible. Because it was an accident there is a chance of being excused of doing this, but I doubt without anyone around that they will believe you. Lapisian in this case should be farwelled in a ceremony that the killer should be the pastor (I guess you can call him that idk I lost a word rn) [Optionally they later could do the same thing they do for shattered capsules where they convert him into energy to empower and stabilize the next generation of Lapisians on working capusles]. Later on Lapisian may experience some psychological stress due to how extreme the situation is. Most likely that Lapisian may become more sensitive to emotional stress in general, basically experiencing a trauma.

Version 4:

Lapisian kills with cold blood

This version is the most terrible one, taking me the most time to think about. Lapisians are generally not going to do anything as such I mentioned above and definitely not this. In this scenario things just cut the line. He dies, you don't care - you die too. There is hope, in persumably psychological rewriting or preforming energy rerolls to check for any abnormalities, because they do NOT kill because they felt like it. If that fails... They will be executed in a pretty calm way, they dont want him to suffer, even though he is a murderer, he doesnt deserve his last moments to be that cruel. Lapisians are not ment to behave like this, they do not tolerate this behavior. Living with a killer is genuienly terrible and they do NOT want anyone like that around. No, there is no workaround (dead Lapisian is farwelled the same way as before).

Ages

Age groups

There's a view differences which happen between different aged lapisians. Everybody gets along very well however there are moments where younger lapisians stand out. Older lapisians are rather calmer and more resilient. They dont take actions too quickly and rather make the right decisions. They have learned from their mistakes from the past, they know what's best, that being repetitiveness, predictability and peace. Younger lapisians are much more energetic and 'rebelious' from the older counterpart. They tend to make more chaotic decisions, they think less, do more which actually may be a good thing, depending on the situation they're in currently. They prefer more differences in life, repetitiveness is boring and predictability is completely unacceptable, they prefer new things which may be more dangerous. Nevertheless they still get on well with eachother, accepting different preferences.
