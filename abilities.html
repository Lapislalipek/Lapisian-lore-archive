Lapisians gain their abilites or so called soul abilities at around time they turn 25. These abilities are necessities for their fragile bodies especially in early years of their life which is going to be quite a challenge to get ahold of. Power is distirbuted only via their hearts which is specifically created for this task in mind. That is part of the reason why it takes most of the time for this very important and complicated life core stays inside the capsules by itself.

Abilites

Supernatural Energy

This Lapisian trait is mainly created to support the other members of the family. They have more strength and delivering the power via power channel is way easier which means they don't have to take breaks that often. Although this power can cause serious harm if used the wrong way. Every soul has its limit to how much energy it contains, adding beyond that point will cause them to feel axious, tired, confused, and overall very very painful in their chests bad due to their heart being literally crushed by the pressure overload and in result if not quickly revailed can be lethal.

Healers (Natural Clearance)

In other words it can clean out wounds aswell as heal other lapisians. Process is rather simple but it takes a lot of power and effort to learn and master. Of course you need more time spent on learning how to heal properly, and as they learn more things they will discover ability to use hosts energy to heal them. Healers posess the ability to calm down other lapisians using their heartbeat, the effect isn't that strong as guardians but it is strong enough for it to be effective. The only need for the effect to work is closeness, lapisian needs to feel safe to be calmed down.

Gem Guardian

Basically the most powerful ability being able to protect and harm too. It lets the owner create structures with lapis lazuli at the cost of energy. This ability is also pretty dangerous in early days of using it because owners have high energy levels inside them and it can harm accidentaly because of the powers not being full controllable. It can shield lapisians fully and it seems to be pretty strong often being enough to stop anyone from even touching these defendless poor souls. But if needeed they will cause harm in the name of protection of their siblings.

Power system is pretty simple, the entire energy is contained within their souls, and the only way out is their heart which can withstand a lot of pressure when releasing the power out, but when energy levels are too low, or they use their powers too much they might feel tired, exhausted or even pass out from the high stress levels. This can be dangerous but not really lethal.

Despite their power to destroy they still posess enough self control to be gentle towards others and will try their best not to harm anyone beyond what's necessary. They also have healing capabilities which are bit more effective and take less time to regenerate. Heartbeat which has been deepened because of the modified body anatomy to enhance energy flow allows them to develop calming capabilities aswell as healers, however this case is more effective, being able to literally put a lapisian to sleep by gettung hugged for long enough.
