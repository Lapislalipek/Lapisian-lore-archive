Content

Underworld creatures

The Underworld creatures are mutated different sized beasts. They're skinny, walk on 4 legs, super agile and dangerous. Their only goal is to eradicate any living creature it its way, and get to the nearest capsule to destroy it. Capsules balance the world and that's why it's their target. These creatures show up about a day after the first signs appear. That can be anything ranging from unnatural behaviors in surroundings to whispers being heard from outside of the hideouts. Guardians heartbeat is being used to save Lapisian lives since if they get too stressed they will fall into a hyperventilation shock and will uncontrollably quckly and shallowly breathe which will result in a faint at least. (In other cases this may be lethal) It is important they don't stress too much or it may end in a very bad way, guardian tends to be much more resistant against any shock, the whole point is for him to stay calm in every situation. He needs to know what to do.

Preparation before encounters

Healers will prepare everything, make sure nobody gets in the way of guardians destructive power, they dont spread chaos, they will mentally support guardian every single time they feel something is off (they do feel it within themselves because of a guardian-healer bond between them, these bonds can be naturally tied aswell between two lapisians alone) hideouts are mainly set up where the capsules (origin of most there living lapisians) are located. Mainly these are cave enterances or stretching a bit away from it. This grants extra safety from other sides.

Biology

Standard energy flow system

Lapisians differ in genetic structure compared to humans, which proves to be quite better. Lapisians hearts being the most important part of the body are connected to an energy vessel, this is where the energy comes out of to flow through the same veins their blood does flow aswell. The energy core produces energy passively and its stored within two containers that with their shape resemble lungs. The capacity increases with the extra years that they gain. Core is a smooth round delicate sphere, and the rest of the energy system is connected with the spirit, it's no longer physical. Energy is the core of their life, that's why Lapisians don't eat that much or need lots of oxygen. Pure energy is much more effective and that's what keeping them alive. That energy is actually shared with their abilities, that's why when a lapisian runs out of the safe energy amounts they get tired or their head starts to spin (in serious causes the body will force the Lapisian to faint, ensuring no more energy comes out). Lapisians can go around 30 minutes without breathing, they still do it uncontrollably with a slower pace compared to humans. Hyperventilation shock is caused by rapid actions and stress bombarding the body. It's not related to suffocation from lack of oxygen.

Energy flow system care

Energy can be recovered the quickest by not doing anything and resting. That way the body regenerates, rests and becomes relaxed (Bonding works the same way however, it amplifies the effects even further). Since Lapisians can't physically interfere with the energy system the only way to check up is using energy. Qualified lapisians such as some Healers and Guardian are able to preform very precise energy injections which exactly go where they require it to. The procedure is super difficult since they must make sure no energy is left behind once they check for everything. It may cause combustions within the body when the patient uses their energy later, energy in wrong places is unstable, like plasma. Most procedures are transferred with physical contact on the spot they want to investigate, so mostly the chest. They place their hand and in a controlled matter release some energy. Their hand being held by the patient ensures that caretaker won't leave anything until they're done with the procedure. They are told to not release until they say they're done and they don't feel anything within them. Checkups are done when necessary or regularily.

Bonding process

Bonding is a process taking part between two Lapisians, which is supposed to ensure they regenerate from day to day. (This subject was mentioned back in Behavior, perks and purpose) This is a special moment for them, it strengthens the connection between them and allows them to understand eachother better. It is carefully polished by the nature to ensure no accidental first connections and that it cannot be interrupted. It has couple of stages to it:

  • Preparation

    The first part contains the initials, Lapisians never accidentally begin to bond, they first need to go through a 4 Stage Safety System (4SS for short) which is the initial border which defines wether they are willing to bond or not:
    • Feelings
      • For bonding to take place Lapisian needs to have a feeling of a strong connection towards their partner.
    • Focus
      • Focus is important, they need to be fully focused on the bonding process, it defines wether they stay together or not.
    • Will
      • Third stage - Lapisian must want to bond, it cannot be done against their will.
    • Shared Conditions
      • Final stage - Both Lapisians must go through the stages before, they both need to have the will to bond, they must be focused and feel connected.
    After 4SS is completed, they must still fulfill an additional requirement, the most important of all, they are required to be in physical contact the entire time, fully connected with eachother, making sure that they are not going to separate through the entire night. This a long process, it takes a lot of time but it really helps and provides fruitful benefits for both. There are risks to improper bonding, but bodies prevent these problems in various ways. Lastly before the step ends they must ensure that they make as little distance between their hearts as possible, so apart from being closely connected (big hugs) they will voluntarily relieve which will relax their bodies giving them extra plasticity on their bodies making them softer. This is often signaled with a deep breath.

  • Beginning

    The second part begins when their bodies do something rather shocking, they force their hearts as far front as possible, making them as close as possible when in contact. This adds to vulnerability they are willing to take even though this isn't going to affect them in any way (unless they get separated). This moment is when things get real, their bodies figure a so called Middle point between eachother, it is the point where their heartbeats and breathing pace are supposed to meet further down ahead. This is the part when they will most likely fall asleep, most of the bonding process actually takes place when they are asleep, they aren't aware of what is happening.

  • Real Bonding

    This is the part when they are not allowed to disconnect anymore. Their hearts take over the whole system at this point, ensuring there is no way they can separate. That includes their minds, heart literally takes over the brain functions temporarily shutting down the brain filling it with different substances that ensure no bad thoughts get into their heads. Because Lapisians can get emotional, bad thoughts can lead to nightmares which could cause them to wake up. The only thing they will dream of will be most likely their partner, only thoughts are about them, idealization of such in their heads. Their hearts begin to adjust overtime to eachother to meet in the Middle point, same for their breathing. While this happens, the main vessel opens up with the heart itself not interrupting the Middle point process. It allows for regeneration of micro damages sustained during the day when using energy. These fractures could become a problem further in the future, that is why preventing these from expanding is important. Another security step towards ensuring they will not wake up on their own is spirit connection. Their souls go into a state where the border between one another disappears, they merge together in some way. It will be the main ingition point for the next step.

  • Completion

    After their vessels fully regenerate, their hearts and breathing adjust, they enter stage of Completion. In this stage they are slowly going to return to their own minds, their merged souls come apart, when they separate in the end, it very gently shocks their bodies, this is the main ignition point for awakening. The vessel closes up, ensuring it doesn't get damaged anymore. Their heads come back to their senses slowly as they regain their normal thinking abilites, hearts leave the brains alone. Once they wake up and disconnect physically their bodies shift their hearts back into their normal position to make sure they will not be vulnerable anymore. When it comes to relieving, they strengthen up with time during the day, it comes by itself. They wake up bonded, adjusted, regenerated and happy they were able to spend the night in that way.
