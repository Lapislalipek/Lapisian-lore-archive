Whole documentation has been divided into multiple pages consisting of different subjects sorted into categories. The main page will contain a brief introduction to the species and its history, while the other pages will contain more detailed information on specific aspects of the species.

Introduction

What are Lapisians?

Lapisians (Lapisius Aurorae) Are the inhabitants of the Azure Lands also reffered on occasions as Lapisia, a plain-like grassy land. Lapisians are a humanoid creatures with a blue color being their main feature as the appearance - blue skin and darker blue hair with occasional appearance of golden-yellow like bits. Lapisians are taller than a normal human being averaging at about 2.4 m in height. They are strongly connected to a spirit mechanism which allows them to gain ablilites which compensate for their natural fragility. To further make up for fragility, Lapisians outlive humans by a large margin, specimens live up to 600 years which gives the opportunity for youngers to be cared for longer despite ridiculous incubation time within Capsules they care for. This is the place where all Lapisians are born.

What are logs about?

Logs follow daily life of a hideout of Lapisians from a perspective of a Lapisian named Lapis, it shows the base perspective, everyday struggles, tragedies and special moments of most important members of the hideout. It shows their feelings, how they handle situations and more. Gem guardian of the hideout is named Laren, he's the main subject of parts of most logs aswell as Larra which additionally shows how valueable these Lapisians are to everyone.

Happy reading!
