Stories

Origins of the rage of the underworld

`When Lapisia was still shaping, the underworld creatures ruled with chaos over these lands. There was no peace, torment but somehow... order? Lapisians were struggling with keeping these creatures away since they were much more invasive than now they are.

The hideouts were slowly getting overrun since guardians couldn't possibly keep up with the amounts of attacks, the late lapsians decided to form a guardian Council across multiple hideouts. Together they forced a tool powerful enough to close the hell gates once and for all, the tool we reffer to currently as The Supernova. Relatively innocent oval shaped blue gem with a spiral in the middle emitting a subtle glow... so innocent yet capable of increasing one's power to wreck chaos beyond imagination. Fully controlled and precise, yet so deadly.

When the plan was set lapisians decided to start closing in together, thus risking lives of others. They knew that was a huge deal but there was nothing they could do apart from that. The first attempt was a complete disaster. They lost dozen of lapisians in the process which put them back on their feet. They were dead worried this may happen again as they had less and less willpower to continue on the plan.

The oldest guardians decided to take matters into their own hands, they rushed the way, completely ignoring everything in their way, with others defending from the back. They barely made it to the hell gates, used the supernova to close the gates once and for all, huge flash has spread across the Lapisia as the gate has closed.

They haven't made it however. The lead of the guardian council have died, over the years everything was slowly falling apart as the council split, everyone slowly forgot about its existence. The hell gates never fully opened again, but the rage of the underworld still remained, getting stronger and stronger as it slightly cracked making a small opening for creatures to crawl out of the underworld... Despite the chaos, lapisians didn't give up and made it past the hard days where they still live til today...`

Suernova

`Relatively innocent oval shaped blue gem with a spiral in the middle emitting a subtle glow... so innocent yet capable of increasing one's power to wreck chaos beyond imagination. Fully controlled and precise, yet so deadly.` As it was mentioned in the legend, Lapisians believe this inconspicuous small relic enhances the effects of any ability hundreads of times on users will. It is said the relic can be used by guardians to eradicate anything within an incomprehensible radius. The tool can cause chaos but also posesses as the only thing within the Lapisian universe to supposedly bring back Lapisians to life. Lapisians aren't sure how it works exactly, since the relic wasn't tested on, it's main purpose was closing interdimensional gates to the underworld (which it did succeed at).
